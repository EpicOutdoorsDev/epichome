Epic Hunt Giveaway Rules

No purchase necessary to enter or win. Please read these Official Rules carefully before entering the Sweepstakes. Epic Outdoors, LLC. (“Sponsor”) will conduct the Epic Outdoors Hunt Giveaway (hereinafter the “Sweepstakes”) substantially as described in these Official Rules, and by participating, each participant agrees to the rules set forth below.

1Description of Sweepstakes

Winning participants will win one of 8 hunting trips. Actual Retail Value (“ARV”) of each prize is approximately $5,000 to $60,000. There is no cash value given to the winners. All winners must pay for travel to and from the hunt location and be willing to legally harvest an animal. ALL hunters must prove they can legally hunt and provide proof of Hunter Safety.

2Dates of Sweepstakes

There are two Sweepstakes periods annually. The “winter sweepstakes” will begin around November 1 and end around February 28 (dates vary from year to year, check epicoutdoors.com for exact dates). The “summer sweepstakes” will begin around April 22 and end around June 23rd. Mail-in entries must be received by no later than 15 days prior to the end date, to allow time for processing.

3How to Enter

Enter three (3) ways: 1. Entrants receive a free entry if they join or refer a new Epic Outdoors membership during the contest period. The new member must mention your name when joining; or 2. By purchasing tickets or 3. By sending in a (“Mail-in Entry”) with a handwritten 3″ X 5″ card. The 3×5 card must include your Full Name, Address, City, State, Zip Code, Phone Number, indicate which hunt the entry is for and mail it to:

Epic Outdoors, LLC.
ATTN: Epic Hunts Giveaway
PO Box 2978
Cedar City, UT 84721

ALL hunters must provide proof of hunter safety. If any hunter cannot prove they are legal to hunt and have passed a hunter safety course, they will forfeit any prizes and receive nothing. Mail-in entries will receive all the same offers and eligibility that purchased entries are entitled to. Sponsor is not responsible for any incomplete, lost, late, illegible, mutilated, or misdirected entries. Sponsor reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to void any and all entries of an entrant who Sponsor believes has attempted to tamper with or impair the administration, fairness, security, or proper play of this sweepstakes, or to cancel the sweepstakes. All entries become the exclusive property of Sponsor and will not be returned or acknowledged.

Winner’s List For a list of winners, mail a self-addressed stamped envelope to Winner’s List to the address above. Mail must be received by the Winner’s List Request Deadline of no later than 12:00 PM noon 15 days after the sweepstakes period. List will be sent once all prizes have been awarded. The winner(s) will also be listed on our website.

No Automated Entries Auto entries prohibited. You must fill out your own entry form without the use of any automated sweepstakes entry filling services or form filling services. The use of any automated, programmed, robotic or similar system to enter is prohibited and will result in disqualification. Entries filled out by anyone other than you will be disqualified.

Limit on Entries The limit on the total number referral entries a person can be rewarded is 8 during the Sweepstakes and the same limit to the number of 3×5 Card Entries for each hunt during the Sweepstakes, as defined herein.

4No Purchase Necessary

You must follow one of the three entry methods to enter.

5Other Requirements

All information must be present. No Purchase necessary. YOU MUST BE AT LEAST 18 (EIGHTEEN) YEARS OF AGE AND A LEGAL RESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OR CANADA TO PARTICIPATE.

Residents of Canada Before being declared an official winner, a potential winner who is a resident of Canada must first, without assistance, correctly answer, a time-limited skill-testing question by telephone within 2 weeks. If a selected entrant cannot be reached or fails to answer the question correctly, he or she will forfeit the opportunity to win and an alternate entrant will be selected as outlined in the official rules.

U.S. laws shall govern this sweepstake. The sweepstake is offered and open to legal residents of the United States (U.S.) and Canada and is void where prohibited. Participants are solely responsible for verifying that they are eligible in their location of residence. Entrants outside of the U.S. are eligible but not eligible to receive physical prizes or alternate prizes. No prizes will be shipped outside of the U.S. Canadian residents must complete a mathematical question administered by Epic Outdoors, per Canadian law. The recipient is solely responsible for any legal requirements in their state or country of origin and is void where prohibited.

If it is determined that a winning entry is not valid, the hunt will be forfeited and awarded to an alternate. In the event that a winner is ineligible, the entrant will be refunded any fees. Entrants must be at least 18 years or older, a licensed driver, and have passed a hunter safety course and have a valid hunter safety number on or before the drawing date. Minors may use a hunt if they are mentored and accompanied by an eligible adult. Sponsor is not responsible for any additional fees incurred by this action. If a mentor arrangement is not possible the hunt is forfeited and awarded to an alternate. By entering, you agree to these Official Rules, which are binding in all respects.

Employees of Sponsor, Epic Outdoors, LLC, and members of the immediate family of any such persons are not eligible to participate and win. The term “immediate family” includes spouses, siblings, parents, children, grandparents, and grandchildren, whether as “in-laws,” or by current or past marriage(s), remarriage(s), adoption, co-habitation or other family extension, and any other persons residing at the same household whether or not related. It is at the sole discretion of the sponsor to determine “competitor” status and reserves the right to refund and disqualify any entries it deems are from competitors, competing individuals, or individuals that would damage the business interests of the sponsor.

The Drawing Winner will be selected by random drawing from eligible entries. Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received. Drawing date will be on or about two days after the promotion period to allow time to process all entries.

Prize Notification Winner will be notified by phone within three (3) business days of the drawing date. If after three (3) attempts by phone during a twenty-four (24) hour time period (as determined by Sponsor in its sole discretion) the Sponsor is unable to contact the winner, the prize will be forfeited and an alternate winner will be selected. Winner must confirm his/her ability to participate in the hunt on the exact dates specified, within 24 hours of being notified. This hunt is non-transferable. If the winner cannot go, the hunt defaults back to Epic Outdoors. If the winner doesn’t complete and return an affidavit of eligibility and liability with a publicity release by two days after the drawing, the winner’s prize will be forfeited. Winner, by accepting these rules, acknowledges and affirms that they meet the sweepstake’s rules eligibility requirements and agree to the publicity/liability set forth below or prize will be forfeited. An alternate winner for forfeited prizes may be selected at sponsor’s sole discretion.
